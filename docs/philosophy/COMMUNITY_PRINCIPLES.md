# Community Principles

> Great software is built by great communities.

SceneForge exists because people choose to share their time, knowledge, and
creativity with one another.

These principles define how we collaborate.

---

# 1. Respect Everyone

Treat every person with respect.

Not because of their experience,
their job title,
their reputation,
or their influence.

Respect people because they are people.

Every contributor deserves to be heard.

---

# 2. Beginners Are Welcome

Everyone writes their first pull request once.

Questions are encouraged.

Mistakes are opportunities to learn.

A welcoming project grows stronger over time.

---

# 3. Ideas Matter More Than Titles

Good ideas can come from anyone.

Architecture should be discussed openly.

Technical decisions should be based on reasoning,
not seniority.

---

# 4. Teach What You Know

Knowledge grows when it is shared.

If you solve a problem,

document it.

If you discover something,

explain it.

If you learn something,

share it.

---

# 5. Leave Things Better

Every contribution should improve the project.

That improvement may be:

- better code
- better documentation
- better tests
- better architecture
- better examples
- better discussions

Small improvements matter.

---

# 6. Review With Kindness

Reviews exist to improve code.

Never to discourage people.

Critique the implementation.

Respect the contributor.

---

# 7. Curiosity Over Ego

Be willing to ask.

Be willing to listen.

Be willing to change your mind.

The best engineering comes from continuous learning.

---

# 8. Build For Everyone

SceneForge should be accessible.

Write clear documentation.

Use understandable names.

Explain complex ideas.

Lower the barrier to contribution.

---

# 9. Respect Life

Technology should improve lives.

We believe every person deserves dignity and respect.

We also believe our work should be mindful of the world around us,
including animals and the environment.

Good engineering considers its broader impact.

---

# 10. Build Something Worth Leaving Behind

Open source is more than code.

It is a gift to future developers.

Write software that teaches.

Design systems that last.

Create knowledge that others can build upon.

---

# Our Culture

We value:

- humility
- curiosity
- craftsmanship
- generosity
- patience
- honesty
- continuous learning

---

# Our Promise

We will strive to make SceneForge a place where people enjoy contributing,
learn from one another,
and build technology that serves others.

---

> "The measure of a project is not only what it builds,
> but how it treats the people who build it."
