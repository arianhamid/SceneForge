# The Ten Commandments of SceneForge

These rules guide every architectural and implementation decision.

---

## I

Thou shalt build knowledge before generation.

---

## II

Thou shalt keep artifacts immutable.

---

## III

Thou shalt isolate providers behind capabilities.

---

## IV

Thou shalt prefer plugins over hardcoded integrations.

---

## V

Thou shalt optimize for understanding rather than demonstrations.

---

## VI

Thou shalt document every public abstraction.

---

## VII

Thou shalt design for replacement, not permanence.

---

## VIII

Thou shalt value simplicity over cleverness.

---

## IX

Thou shalt think in ecosystems, not applications.

---

## X

Thou shalt leave the codebase better than thou found it.
